Install it in three steps

  1. Download

    Tap Download for Android on your phone. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, choose Download anyway. Browsers ask this about every app that does not come from Google Play.

  2. Install

    Open the file when it has downloaded. The first time, Android asks whether your browser may install apps: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install. If Google Play Protect offers to scan the app, let it.

  3. Sign in

    Open DeKoi and sign in with your Google account. The app then shows you how to set up your DeKoi, one step at a time.

Prepare the memory card on a computer

The app can prepare your DeKoi's memory card with a USB card reader. You can also do it with DeKoi Setup on a Windows computer.

Inside the app

Once your DeKoi is plugged in, the app shows how your network is doing in plain words. Quiet days stay quiet. When something touches the decoy, you get an alert with clear steps.

Updates

To update the app, download it here again and install it over the old one. Your account and your DeKoi stay as they are.
