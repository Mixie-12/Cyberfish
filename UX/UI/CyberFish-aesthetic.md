# CyberFish aesthetic guide

Dark, calm, minimal. Neon blue is an accent, never a fill. Energy comes from clarity, not decoration.

## Fonts

| Font | Use | Get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Michroma** (Regular) | Wordmarks (CYBERFISH, DeKoi), headings, section numbers | fonts.google.com/specimen/Michroma |
| **JetBrains Mono** (Regular 400, Medium 500, ExtraBold 800) | Body text, labels, UI, captions. ExtraBold only for the logo | fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono |

Rules
- Michroma only big: wordmarks, headings and section numbers. Headings in UPPERCASE, tracking about 0.02em.
- "DeKoi" is always mixed case, exactly `DeKoi`.
- JetBrains Mono for everything else. Paragraphs in sentence case, max 3 to 4 lines.
- Labels and HUD text: JetBrains Mono, small, UPPERCASE, wide tracking (0.12 to 0.14em), e.g. `01 / THE PROBLEM`.

| White | `#F2F4FA` | Headings, wordmark |

| Dim grey | `#4A5368` | Captions, HUD labels |

Blue covers at most about 10% of any screen. Everything else is dark.

## Logo

- The mark is `><>` (a fish) set in JetBrains Mono ExtraBold, tracking -0.06em, white fill with a CyberFish blue stroke.
- Glow version for large and hero use only; flat version under about 48 px tall.
- Lockup: mark at the cap height of the text next to it, gap 0.6em. Never taller than the text.

## Visual motifs

- **HUD frame:** one thin line around the edge (blue at about 15% opacity) with small 45-degree cut corners. Nothing else on it.
- **Horizon:** a glowing horizontal line with faint lines below it that spread out toward the bottom, like a dark sea. Use on title and statement slides, with the mark floating above it and its reflection below.
- **Hairlines:** thin horizontal lines carry structure (timelines, dividers, lists) instead of boxes and cards.
- Lots of empty space. One idea per slide or section.
- Avoid: boxes around everything, gradients on text, stock "cyber" fonts (Orbitron, Audiowide), bright neon fills.

## Voice

Calm, specific, plain language. No exclamation marks. No hype words (seamless, robust, powerful, cutting-edge, leverage, revolutionary). No fear-mongering.
